## Scope (v2.0)

This policy applied to company-owned equipment only. Leased units were
maintained by the lessor and were not part of the register.

## Tier assignment rules (v2.0)

| # | Condition | Maintenance Tier |
|---|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | `age_years` is 7 or more | Overhaul |
| 2 | `age_years` is 4 to 6 | Major Service |
| 3 | `age_years` is 2 to 3 | Standard Service |
| 4 | `age_years` is below 2 | Baseline Check |

The `duty` field was not used for tiering under this version.
